ABOUT:
BlackTieEvent.app is an Event Management and Fundraising Platform built to do two things: give you everything you need to plan and run your event, and everything you need to raise money on event night. From ticket sales, seating charts, and guest check-in to live auctions, raffles, sponsorships, and real-time donations, it connects every moving part of your event into one streamlined system. Instead of juggling spreadsheets, separate tools, and disconnected payment platforms, everything operates inside one simple dashboard designed for clarity, speed, and control. The result is a smoother guest experience, less stress for your team, and a structure intentionally built to maximize revenue without adding complexity.

KEY FEATURES:

SIMPLE, INTUITIVE Back end OPERATING SYSTEM THAT WORKS ON ANY DEVICE
{% for img in row_1_image_list %}
{% endfor %}
The system is designed to feel immediately familiar, so you’re not learning software — you’re just using it. Everything is organized into clear, purpose-built apps, making event management feel simple instead of technical. When you select an event, the entire platform switches into that context — databases, images, schedules, tickets, seating, auctions, and live views all update specifically for that event. …

Each app behaves as if it exists only for that event, eliminating cross-event confusion and manual cleanup. You are never copying data, re-uploading assets, or wondering which settings apply where. Everything stays isolated, organized, and intentionally scoped to the event you’re working on.

This structure allows teams to move quickly and confidently. Staff can jump between ticket sales, seating, auctions, raffles, check-in, and reporting without changing tools or mental context. Updates made in one place immediately reflect everywhere else, keeping the system consistent and reliable throughout planning, setup, and event day.

The experience works seamlessly across iPhone, desktop, Mac, tablet, and web, so staff, volunteers, and guests can all use the platform on the devices they already have. There is no training period, no learning curve, and no technical barrier. People intuitively understand how to navigate the system, which keeps teams focused on running the event instead of managing software.

Three Built in fundraising apps to Maximize Event Day Revenue: Silent Auction, Raffles, Live Auction
{% for img in row_2_image_list %}
{% endfor %}
The platform is built around multiple integrated revenue streams that are designed to work together, not compete with each other. You operate from a public front-end event website where you can sell individual tickets, full tables, and custom sponsorship levels before the event even begins. Sponsor logos and placements appear across the website, printed materials, and event-facing screens, creating early visibility and sellable exposure. …

In addition to ticket and sponsorship sales, the platform supports general donations from supporters who simply want to give, allowing contributions outside of auctions or raffles without friction or manual handling.

On the event side, the system includes three core revenue experiences: silent auctions, raffle items, and live auctions. Each operates as its own dedicated app with independent pages, schedules, pricing rules, and bidding logic, while remaining fully connected to the same event, guests, and reporting.

Every auction and raffle item automatically generates a print-ready display card that includes photos, item details, and a QR code linking directly to the correct live bidding or raffle page. With a single click, all cards are bundled into a single printable PDF, whether you have five items or sixty. Guests scan once and are taken instantly to the correct page to bid, buy, or enter, eliminating staff involvement and delays.

During the event, the platform’s display mode runs on TVs and projectors, showcasing live auction items, current prices, countdowns, and rotating sponsor placements. These screens are often sold as premium sponsorship opportunities, creating an additional revenue stream that many events use to offset or fully cover platform costs.

Sponsor visibility continues throughout the experience. A live sponsor ticker runs across auction, raffle, and live pages, as well as display screens, keeping sponsors visible wherever guests are engaging. These placements are sellable, measurable, and persist across both web and event-night displays.

Payments can be completed directly by guests on their own devices or by staff on the back end when needed, using secure, bank-level processing. All revenue streams stay fully connected to receipts, reporting, winner confirmation, and live status updates, ensuring nothing is lost, duplicated, or manually reconciled.

BUILT-IN EVENT WEBSITE DESIGNED FOR PROMOTION, TICKET SALES, AND DONATIONS
{% for img in row_3_image_list %}
{% endfor %}
The platform includes a front-end event website with a built-in edit mode, allowing you to instantly switch between viewing the site exactly as guests see it and making live updates behind the scenes. Editing works the way people expect — upload images, update text, adjust colors, rearrange sections, and control visibility — with changes that take effect immediately across the event website and all related ticket and purchase pages. …

The event website can link back to your main organization website, or it can run as a fully standalone event site with its own navigation and branding. You control whether guests see a back button, where links point, and how the site is shared. The site can be promoted publicly, sent directly to guests, or embedded into email campaigns and text messages to support pre-event promotion and outreach.

From this front-end website, guests can buy sponsorships, purchase full tables, or buy individual tickets from a single, unified page. Pricing, quantity limits, seat counts, and availability are all managed from the backend, with real-time inventory tracking to prevent overselling.

Payments are processed through Stripe, providing bank-level security, fraud protection, and compliance out of the box.

After purchase, guests immediately receive a confirmation email containing their tickets, including a PDF with a unique QR code.

Sponsor logos, images, descriptions, and links are managed centrally and appear consistently across the event website, ticket pages, auction pages, raffle pages, and optional on-site display screens.

The same website content feeds into printed materials, QR codes, and live event experiences, ensuring branding, pricing, and messaging stay consistent before, during, and after the event.
